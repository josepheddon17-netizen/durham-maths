
\section{2D Surfaces}

\begin{definition}[2D Surface]
  A 2-d surface embedded in $\mathbb{R}^3$ can be defined parametrically as a map $\boldsymbol{x}(u, v)$ from $\mathbb{R}^2 \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^3$, for $u \in\left[u_0, u_1\right]$ and $v \in\left[v_0, v_1\right]$. For each value of $v$, the position $\boldsymbol{x}(u, v)$ describes a curve on the surface with tangent vector $\frac{\partial \boldsymbol{x}}{\partial u}$.
\end{definition}
